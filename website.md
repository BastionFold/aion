## Aion

**Status:** In development

Boardroom-grade multi-model AI that runs entirely on your own hardware — zero cloud fees, full privacy, works offline. Aion runs a council of local models (Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral) that review each other's work, right on your machine.

### What's new
- Local "voicemail" councils — multiple models deliberating with nothing sent to the cloud
- Hardware-aware model selection that fits the machine you're on
